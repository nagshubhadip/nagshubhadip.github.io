About Me

Hello! I am Shubhadip Nag, currently working as a Data Scientist 3 at Walmart Global Tech, Bangalore, focusing on large-scale machine learning and data science solutions for Price Optimization. I completed my MS (Research) from IIT Kharagpur under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Sourangshu Bhattacharya, CNeRG Lab, Department of Computer Science and Engineering. My research focuses on LLM unlearning, data subset selection, trustworthy AI, and explainable AI.

Beyond research, I enjoy exploring the applications of AI in education and social media analysis, and finding ways to make AI more inclusive and reliable for diverse communities. Outside of work, I am an avid cricket player and enjoy travelling.
